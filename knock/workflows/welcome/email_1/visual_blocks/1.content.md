# Welcome to CommonGrid!

Thanks for joining us. CommonGrid is the open registry of US energy infrastructure — utilities, power plants, transmission lines, EV charging stations, grid operators, and more. All free, all open source, all driven by people like you.

## What you can do here

**Explore the data**  
Browse thousands of utilities, power plants, and grid operators. Filter by state, type, capacity, or fuel source. Every entity on the map is backed by real data from EIA filings, HIFLD, and community contributions.

**Make contributions**  
See something wrong or incomplete? You can suggest edits to any entity. Add missing information, correct outdated data, or create new records. Every contribution helps make the grid data more accurate and useful.

**How contributions work**  
When you submit an edit, our volunteer moderators review it to ensure data quality. They'll either approve your change, request clarifications, or provide feedback. You'll get an email notification at each step, so you'll always know the status of your contribution.

We love contributions from the community — they're what make CommonGrid the best-in-class dataset for US energy infrastructure. Even small corrections matter.
